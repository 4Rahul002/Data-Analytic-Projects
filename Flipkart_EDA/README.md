# **Project Summary**

This project aims to conduct an in-depth analysis of customer support interactions at Flipkart, one of India’s leading e-commerce platforms. Effective customer service is a critical factor in maintaining customer trust and loyalty. With increasing competition in the e-commerce space, providing timely and satisfactory responses to customer queries is essential for sustaining long-term growth. This analysis focuses on evaluating customer satisfaction, agent performance, and the operational efficiency of Flipkart’s customer support system.

The dataset under review contains over 85,000 records spanning multiple customer interaction channels, including inbound calls, outbound calls, and chat-based support. It encompasses detailed attributes such as customer inquiries, agent information, customer satisfaction scores (CSAT), and the time taken to respond to issues. By analyzing these variables, the project seeks to uncover patterns in customer sentiment, identify operational bottlenecks, and provide actionable insights to optimize customer service delivery.

The primary aim of the project is to improve overall customer satisfaction and operational efficiency. Customer satisfaction is measured through CSAT scores on a scale of 1 to 5, with 5 representing the highest level of satisfaction. Analyzing these scores across different product categories and support channels allows us to identify areas where service falls short. Additionally, assessing agent performance based on handling time, tenure, and shift patterns provides critical insights into productivity and customer engagement quality.

Another key aspect of the analysis involves evaluating unresolved customer issues. Delays in issue resolution or repeat inquiries indicate gaps in service quality. By identifying patterns in these cases, we can provide recommendations to improve first-call resolution rates and minimize unresolved tickets. Furthermore, understanding customer concerns across different categories, such as returns, cancellations, and product queries, enables the formulation of targeted strategies to address the most frequent pain points.

A thorough analysis of handling times across various agent shifts helps identify when customer service is most effective and where inefficiencies lie. This is particularly useful for optimizing workforce allocation and ensuring that peak hours are adequately staffed. The dataset also allows for the segmentation of performance by agent tenure, which provides insights into whether more experienced agents deliver better outcomes or if there is room for improved training across all experience levels.

By leveraging advanced data analysis techniques and visualization, this project offers a comprehensive view of customer support operations. The ultimate goal is to reduce handling times, enhance customer satisfaction, and ensure that the majority of customer issues are resolved on the first interaction. These insights will support Flipkart in refining its customer support strategies, improving service quality, and maintaining a competitive edge in the e-commerce landscape.

# **Problem Statement**:

Customer satisfaction is a key driver for Flipkart's continued growth. However, inconsistencies in handling time, agent performance, and unresolved customer issues may impact the overall customer experience. This analysis aims to identify performance gaps, analyze customer sentiment, and recommend actionable solutions to improve customer service.

# **Business Objective**:

Improve customer satisfaction (CSAT) by identifying key drivers of low satisfaction.

Optimize agent performance by analyzing handling time and shift-based productivity.

Streamline customer support operations by reducing unresolved cases and improving first-call resolution.

Identify trends in customer issues across different product categories and channels.

# **Variable Descriptions:**

Unique ID - Unique identifier for each support interaction.

Channel Name - Customer interaction method (Inbound, Outcall, Chat).

Category & Sub-category - Broad and detailed issue classification (e.g., Returns, Product Queries).

Customer Remarks - Customer feedback post-interaction.

Order ID - Identifier for the order associated with the inquiry.

Order Date & Issue Reported Time - Time-related variables for interaction tracking.

Agent Information - Agent name, supervisor, manager, tenure, and shift.

CSAT Score - Customer Satisfaction Score (1-5 scale).

# **Solution To bUsiness Objective**

Use statistical analysis and visualization to detect patterns in customer satisfaction.

Evaluate agent performance by analyzing handling times and customer feedback.

Identify high-issue categories and assess the resolution effectiveness.

Develop actionable recommendations to reduce handling time and improve first-call resolution.

# ** Conclusion **

A data-driven analysis of customer support operations provides actionable insights to enhance customer satisfaction, optimize agent performance, and improve operational efficiency. By addressing key gaps in handling time and unresolved issues, Flipkart can maintain a competitive edge in delivering superior customer service.
